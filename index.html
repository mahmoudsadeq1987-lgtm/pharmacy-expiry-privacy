pharmacy expiry backup

About This Application

pharmacy expiry backup is a desktop application designed to help pharmacy managers and staff track medication expiry dates, manage product catalogs, and maintain compliance records. The application allows users to:

  • Add and manage pharmaceutical products with expiry dates and pricing
  • Track product expiry status (valid, expiring soon, expired)
  • Generate barcode/QR labels for products
  • Scan products and generate invoices
  • Back up and restore product data locally or via Google Drive
  • Sync product catalogs from a shared Google Drive folder across multiple devices

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Information We Collect

pharmacy expiry backup stores all product and scan data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal information or usage analytics.

Google Drive Integration

The application optionally integrates with Google Drive for two features:

  • Cloud Backup: If you enable Cloud Backup, your product and scan data is saved to your personal Google Drive account as encrypted backup files. We do not have access to your Google Drive data.
  • Cloud Sync: If you enable Cloud Sync, the application reads product catalog files (Excel or JSON) from a shared Google Drive folder to update your local product list. Only file names and content from the designated shared folder are accessed.

We request the minimum Google Drive permissions necessary: the ability to create, read, and update files created by this application, and to list files in a designated shared folder.

Data Storage

All application data is stored locally on your device in an SQLite database. No data is sent to any external servers. Google Drive integration, if used, stores data solely in your own Google Drive account.

Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your data. All data is stored locally on your device unless you explicitly enable cloud features. Cloud backups are stored in your personal Google Drive account and are protected by your Google account security.

Third-Party Services

The application uses the Google Drive API solely for optional cloud backup and sync features. No other third-party services are used. No advertising or analytics SDKs are included.

Children's Privacy

This application is intended for professional use by pharmacy staff and is not directed at children under 13.
